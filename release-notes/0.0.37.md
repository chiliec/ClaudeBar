### Fixes a false "Setup ClaudeBar" screen after upgrading

If a session expired while you were still on 0.0.35, that version deleted the
account and cleared the active-account pointer. Upgrading to 0.0.36 then showed
the first-run Setup screen even though your other accounts were still saved,
and the only way back was to sign in again.

ClaudeBar now falls back to the first saved account when the pointer is
missing, so your accounts show up straight away.
