### Supports Ultra accounts without a 5-hour window

On 30 October Anthropic renamed Max 20x to Ultra and removed the rolling
5-hour limit for verified Ultra accounts. ClaudeBar kept drawing an empty
"5-Hour Window" bar at 0% for those accounts.

The big bar now shows the 7-day total when there is no 5-hour window, so the
panel headline matches the menu bar ring. Accounts that still have a 5-hour
window look the same as before.

Tier badges follow the rename: "Ultra" and "Max" instead of "Max 20×" and
"Max 5×".
