## What's new in v0.0.22

**Fixes a silent failure when connecting your session key**

If your Claude.ai account exposes more than one organization but only one of them is a paid/trackable plan (a very common setup — a personal org alongside your paid workspace), pasting your `sessionKey` and hitting Connect did nothing: no usage, no organization picker, no error. ClaudeBar now connects the single paid organization automatically in that case, shows a picker whenever there's a real choice, and surfaces a clear message when an account has no organizations at all — so connecting never dead-ends silently again.
