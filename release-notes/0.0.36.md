### An expired account no longer locks you out of the others

When one account's Claude session expired, ClaudeBar dropped it and showed a
full-screen "Sign in" panel with no way to reach your other accounts. The only
way out was to re-authorize that exact account.

The expired account now stays in the account menu, marked "expired", and the
menu stays in the header above the sign-in panel. Switch to any other account
and usage loads as usual; pick the expired one and you get the sign-in panel
straight away, no waiting on a doomed request. Signing in again replaces the
expired entry in place.
