### Multiple accounts

Sign in to more than one Claude.ai account and switch between them:

- **Account switcher** in the popover header — click your email to change the
  active account.
- **Settings** lists every signed-in account with a per-account Remove button.
- Existing sign-ins migrate automatically; no action needed.

### Menu bar polish

- The status item is narrower and the label is centered.
- The panel no longer shifts or resizes while switching accounts or when the
  percentage changes width.
