### Fixes the clipped panel in 0.0.33

0.0.33 left the panel stuck at a fixed height, cutting the header off the top
and the rest off the bottom. Its background view was measured before it had a
size, so the interface drawn on top of it was laid out to nothing and then
scaled up from there.

That release also described the sign-in crash wrongly. The crash came from
letting the panel resize itself from inside its own layout pass, not from where
the background was drawn — 0.0.33 happened to stop doing that, which is why it
fixed the crash. Both are now fixed deliberately, and the panel sizes itself to
its content again.

The release process now opens the panel and checks it before shipping. Every
release since 0.0.30 was broken in the panel, and nothing opened it until you
clicked.
